# Writing and layout review

The report uses five pages, one per supplied challenge question. Tables preserve the historical benchmark values for all seven algorithms. The revision uses direct explanations of charging rules and results, without promotional claims or treating a higher number as proof of validity.

The final review checked three distinctions: daily participants versus simultaneous full-power charging; deadline shortfall versus grid overload; and electrical concentration versus workplace/public behavior. The evidence-status note stays on page one. The final text avoids Unicode dashes and unsupported real-city maxima. All five rendered pages were inspected for clipping, table layout, spacing and legibility.
